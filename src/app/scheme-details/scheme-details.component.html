Important Payment Information

We understand that life can be unpredictable. Here is how delayed payments are handled.

📆

Payment Date Adjustment

If you miss a monthly payment, your remaining payment schedule will be adjusted based on the actual date you make the delayed payment.

🔄

Scheme Duration Extension

Any delay in payment will extend the remaining scheme duration accordingly. Your maturity date will be adjusted based on the revised schedule.

₹

No Additional Delay Charges

No additional charges or penalties apply solely because of a delayed instalment.

❤️

Regular Payments Are Better

We encourage you to pay on time every month to complete your scheme smoothly without unnecessary delays.
